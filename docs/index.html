Scouting in Rockville Centre since 1914

Troop 40 · Boy Scouts of Rockville Centre

Adventure, friendship, and leadership, one campout at a time.

Troop 40 is a Scout-led Scouts BSA troop for boys in Rockville Centre (RVC), Long Island, sponsored by United Church of RVC. Our Scouts learn to camp, hike, cook, lead, and serve their community. We've been doing it for more than a century, and there's room around the campfire for you.

What we do

Scouting is learning by doing. Our Scouts plan their own trips, earn merit badges, and gain real skills they'll use for life.

🥾

Hiking & Camping

Monthly campouts and day hikes all year round, from fall foliage trips to winter cabin weekends.

🤝

Service Projects

Food drives, park cleanups, and Eagle Scout projects that give back to Rockville Centre.

🏅

Rank & Merit Badges

A clear path from Scout to Eagle, with dozens of merit badges in everything from first aid to robotics.

🧭

Leadership

Scouts run the troop. They lead patrols, plan meetings, and teach younger Scouts.

Summer Camp

A week at Onteora Scout Reservation

Every summer we head to Onteora in the Catskills for a week of swimming, canoeing, archery, rifle and shotgun shooting, merit badge classes, and campfires under the stars. For many of our Scouts it's the highlight of the year.

What Scouting builds

The skills last well beyond the campsite.

  • Confidence. Trying new things and finding out they can do hard things.
  • Character. Living the Scout Oath and Law every day.
  • Self-reliance. Cooking, navigating, first aid, and being prepared.
  • Friendship. Lifelong bonds made on the trail and around the fire.
  • Citizenship. Serving their town, their country, and each other.
  • Leadership. Real responsibility, with adult mentors to back them up.

Join Troop 40

Looking for Boy Scouts in Rockville Centre? New Scouts are welcome all year, and no experience is needed. The best first step is to visit a meeting.

Who can join

Boys ages 11 to 17, or 10 after finishing fifth grade or earning the Arrow of Light in Cub Scouts.

When & where we meet

Tuesday evenings during the school year, at Wilson Elementary School or United Church of RVC in Rockville Centre.

Part of Scouting America

Troop 40 is a Scouts BSA troop in the Long Island Council of Scouting America, formerly the Boy Scouts of America.

How to get started

Email the troop and a leader will help you find the next meeting and walk you through signing up.
